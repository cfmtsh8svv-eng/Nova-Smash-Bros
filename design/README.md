# Design

The pictures the team chose from before and during the build. Nothing in this folder is served or used by the game; open the HTML files straight from the folder in a browser.

| Folder | What is in it |
|---|---|
| [`final/`](final/) | The final art reference: `reference.html` shows the fighters and stages drawn by `characters.js` and `stages.js`. The game uses ports of these in `public/shared/art/`, and its platforms come from `src/game/stages.js`. |
| [`options/`](options/) | The option rounds 1 to 11 (art style, characters, stages, HUD, phone, intro, podium). The decisions they led to are in [`docs/DECISIONS.md`](../docs/DECISIONS.md). Rounds 10 and 11 load the game's own art from `public/`. |
| [`mockup/`](mockup/) | The first visual mock-up of the concept phase (D-026). Historical: it shows ideas that were later changed. |
