Game concept · Mock-up v0.2

NVA BRAWL

Scan. Pick. Brawl.

A party platform fighter set at Nova SBE. Up to six players scan a QR code and their phones become controllers. Every hit raises your damage %, high % sends you flying, and at 200 % you burn out.

  • 2–6players
  • 3stages
  • 3lives each
  • 200 %burnout
  • 0app installs

How a round works

From QR code to graduation in four steps

  1. 1

    Scan

    The big screen shows a QR code. Scan it with your phone. No app, no account needed.

  2. 2

    Pick

    Type your name, choose one of six fighters and press Ready.

  3. 3

    Brawl

    Hits raise your %. The higher it is, the further you fly. Three lives each, and 200 % means burnout.

  4. 4

    Graduate

    The last player standing graduates with honours. Awards, stats, rematch.

Screens

Big screen and phone

Click through the main screens. On the phone controller, tap a direction and then A or B to see which move comes out.

Roster · names are placeholders

Six student archetypes

Same controls for everyone, different moves. Each fighter has a signature colour and a silhouette you can recognise from across the room.

Stages

Campus, beach, Bairro Alto

One hazard at a time, every 25–40 seconds, always with a 3-second warning on the big screen and on every phone.

Items

Tarts, books and one very large sardine

Items spawn every 12–20 seconds, three at most. Walk over one and press A.

Hybrid KO system

How far do you fly?

Launch speed grows with your damage %, and heavier fighters fly less. Reaching 200 % is an instant KO. Move the slider to see it. The values are starting points for playtesting.

120 %
    speed = (base + growth × % / 100) × (100 / weight)

    "Likely KO" from 16 px per frame is a rough guess for a 1280 × 720 stage. It will be tuned in playtests.
